# Patent / R&D Direction

The broad idea of an AI health wearable is crowded.

A stronger technical thesis is an adaptive edge health-risk engine combining:
- personal baseline
- physiological signals
- activity/context
- environment
- temporal risk trajectory
- signal quality
- graceful degradation
- offline edge inference

Maintain a prior-art matrix, experiment logs, dataset provenance, model versions, hardware revisions and architecture decisions.

Patentability must be evaluated against prior art and applicable Indian law, with professional patent review before filing.
