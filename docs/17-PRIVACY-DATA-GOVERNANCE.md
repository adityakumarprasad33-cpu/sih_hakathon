# Privacy and Data Governance

## Principles
- data minimization
- purpose limitation
- explicit access
- transparency
- retention limits
- secure deletion
- audited privileged access

## Data
Account/profile, health measurements, risk outputs, device telemetry, doctor consent, emergency contacts and profile images.

## Retention
Define separate policies for raw research data, derived telemetry, risk history, alerts, audit events and account data.

## Deletion
Document deletion/retention behavior for Firebase, Cloudinary, device storage, app cache and backups.

Before commercialization, obtain India-specific privacy, medical-device and regulatory review based on actual claims and functionality.
